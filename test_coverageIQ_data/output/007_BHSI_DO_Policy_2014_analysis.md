Status: OPTIMIZATION
Confidence: High

Finding: No provision in Section IV (Exclusions) is captioned as a Pollution Exclusion, but pollution-related cleanup/remediation costs are excluded through the definition of "Loss" itself, which carves out amounts incurred to test for, monitor, clean up, remove, contain, treat, detoxify, or neutralize a broadly defined "Pollutants."
Recommendation: Consider requesting removal of the Pollution Exclusion.
Evidence (Loss Carve-Out): "\"Loss\" shall mean those amounts any Insured is legally obligated to pay as a result of a Claim... Loss (other than Defense Costs) shall not include any of the following: ... iv. any amount incurred to test for, monitor, clean up, remove, contain, treat, detoxify or neutralize Pollutants;" (Page 5, Section III(14))
Evidence (Pollutants Definition): "\"Pollutants\" shall mean any substance located anywhere in the world exhibiting any hazardous characteristics as defined by, or identified on any list of hazardous substances issued by, the United States Environmental Protection Agency or any state, county, municipality or locality counterpart thereof including, but not limited to, nuclear material or nuclear waste. Such substances shall include, without limitation, solids, liquids, gaseous, biological, radiological or thermal irritants, contaminants or smoke, vapor, dust, fibers, mold, spores, fungi, germs, soot, fumes, acids, alkalis, chemicals or waste materials and any other air emission, odor, waste water, oil or oil products, infectious or medical waste, asbestos or asbestos products and any noise." (Page 7, Section III(19))
Reasoning: This is a case where the exclusionary mechanism lives in the Definitions section rather than the Exclusions section — recognizable only by its effect (removing pollution cleanup costs from what qualifies as recoverable "Loss"), not by a "Pollution Exclusion" heading. No endorsement modifying or deleting this provision was identified in the document provided, so it remains operative. Note it is narrower than a full liability exclusion for pollution-related Wrongful Acts — it specifically removes cleanup/remediation-type costs from Loss rather than excluding pollution-related claims outright.
